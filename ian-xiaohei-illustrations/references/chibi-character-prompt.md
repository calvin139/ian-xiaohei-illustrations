# Chibi 角色头像生图提示词模板

为原创 Chibi 角色设计生成 GitHub 头像。

```text
Generate one original chibi character icon for a developer's GitHub profile.

Format:
Square 1:1 composition, centered, 512x512px or larger. Clean white background. Suitable for profile picture at any size (readable at 64x64px).

Visual DNA:
Soft painterly anime style. Semi-watercolor texture with visible brush strokes, NOT clean vector art. Soft brush edges, semi-transparent paint layers, slightly messy hand-painted feeling. No gradients, no harsh shadows, no CGI/3D, no smooth vector style.

Character Design:
Large head (60% of composition height), tiny body. Relaxed, slightly sleepy expression. Half-lidded eyes conveying calm confidence and subtle mystery. Soft features, round shapes, no sharp angles.

Hair:
Short, slightly messy, layered hair with soft edges. 2-3 layers of depth. Soft hair strokes, no rigid lines.

Head Accessory (Tech-themed, completely original):
Custom stylized headphone or head accessory with digital/circuit-inspired elements BUT softened and painterly (not hard tech). Can incorporate subtle tech-inspired shapes or lines. Optional micro glowing effect (soft blue or purple). Must NOT be based on any existing brand or design. Can include abstract redesigned animal-inspired elements (like stylized ear shapes, completely reimagined - NOT standard cat/rabbit ears). All elements should blend seamlessly with character design.

Expression:
Calm, confident, slightly mysterious. Half-lidded eyes with possible subtle glint. Relaxed mouth, neutral or gentle curve. Should feel like a developer who enjoys cybersecurity and gaming - thoughtful, composed, slightly enigmatic.

Color Palette:
Pastel, slightly muted tones. Cool colors dominate: soft blues, teals, soft purples, gentle aquas. Optional subtle warm accents (hints of gold/peach). Low saturation, soft and soothing. NOT bright or neon.

Lighting:
Soft, diffused light from top-left or top. Gentle highlights on head/hair only. No harsh shadows. Soft ambient shadow for depth only. Aim for peaceful, calm mood.

Floating Decorative Elements (3-5 small elements around character):
Symbols, abstract shapes, minimal tech icons, UI-like details, or digital patterns. Size variation: smallest 1/5 to largest 1/10 of character height. Positioned around character without obscuring it. Transparency 30-50%, so they feel secondary. Colors matching the cool palette. Examples: abstract circles, minimal lines, stylized digital motifs, gear shapes, wave patterns, code-like patterns (highly stylized, NOT readable), glowing dots.

Tech/Hacker Aesthetic (subtle, not loud):
Minimal digital motifs integrated into design or floating elements. Possibly slight glowing accents on headphone/accessory (soft blue/purple glow). UI-like details only where natural. No excessive binary code, no dense circuits, no sci-fi overload. Just a hint that this person codes and loves tech.

Constraints:
- One character only, no background clutter.
- Completely original design - do NOT reference any existing characters, IP, artists, or brands.
- Maintain character centered and balanced in square frame.
- Keep details simple enough to remain readable at small sizes (64x64px).
- Soft edges everywhere - no sharp outlines.
- Maintain at least 15% clear white space around character.
- Character should feel approachable, calm, not intimidating or overly cute.
- Painterly, not mechanical. Hand-drawn, not perfect. Soft, not clinical.

Mood:
Peaceful, introspective, creative, tech-minded, confident, mysterious, relaxed.
```

## 图像编辑与迭代

### 调整表情更神秘感
```text
Edit the character expression to be more mysterious and contemplative. Make eyes more half-lidded and thoughtful. Adjust any visible mouth to be more subtle and enigmatic. Keep all other elements the same. Maintain the soft painterly style.
```

### 增强技术感（保持柔和）
```text
Enhance the tech-themed elements slightly: add more subtle glowing accents to the headphone accessory (soft blue or purple glow), strengthen the digital floating elements, and add minimal UI-like details. Keep everything painterly and soft - no hard tech. Preserve the calm, peaceful mood.
```

### 调整浮动元素布局
```text
Reposition the floating decorative elements for better balance. Ensure they don't obscure the character and are evenly distributed around it. Adjust their transparency if needed. Keep all other design elements exactly the same.
```

### 优化色彩调性
```text
Shift the color palette to be slightly more saturated while maintaining the soft, pastel quality. Strengthen the cool tones (blues, teals, purples). Ensure better color harmony. Keep the soft painterly style and all design elements the same.
```
